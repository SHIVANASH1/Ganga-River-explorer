The Journey

Pollution Analysis

BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand) is the key metric. Acceptable limit: 3 mg/L. Kanpur and Varanasi breach safe limits by 15–20x. Namami Gange (₹20,000 Cr project) has been working since 2014 to reverse this.

Kanpur — Most Critical City
400+ tanneries dump chromium-laced wastewater directly. Leather industry generates ~50 MLD toxic effluent daily. BOD reaches 65 mg/L — 21x above safe limit. Jajmau drain alone carries sewage from 5 lakh people.

Tributary Rivers

Ganga grows mightier at every confluence. These rivers — some crystal-clean from glaciers, some carrying city pollution — join the main stem across 2,500 km.

Fish Species

The Ganga basin hosts 140+ fish species. Clean upper reaches harbour Mahseer and Golden Mahseer; the middle Gangetic plains are rich in Rohu and Catla; the Gangetic Dolphin (Susu) is India's National Aquatic Animal.

Depth & Width

Depth varies dramatically — from ankle-deep mountain streams to 35m channels near Patna. Monsoon (June–Sept) triples average depth everywhere.

Flood Zones

Every monsoon, Ganga and its tributaries inundate vast stretches of the plains. Bihar alone loses thousands of crores annually to floods. The Kosi river — called "Sorrow of Bihar" — shifts its course by 100+ km in severe floods.
